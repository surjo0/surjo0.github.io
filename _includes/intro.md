Hi! I am Surjo, graduated from the Department of Computer Science at **Rajiv Gandhi Institute of Petroleum Technology**. I completed my Secondary and Higher Secondary education in Bangladesh before pursuing my bachelor's degree in India. I am currently planning to pursue a PhD in the areas of Computer Vision, Vision-Language Models, and Generative AI.

During my bachelor's degree, I worked with Prof. [Susham Biswas](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=l1T71wcAAAAJ&hl=en) on research focused on structural deformation identification and analysis using machine learning. I also worked with Dr. [Pallabi Saikia](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=JmNyqQUAAAAJ&hl=en) on theoretical and computational analysis of convergence and explainability in diffusion-based generative models for Vision AI.

After Graduation I worked on Vision-Language Models under the supervision of Dr. [Vinay Kumar Verma](https://scholar.google.com/citations?hl=en&user=7x6GZ1EAAAAJ) (Senior Applied Scientist at Amazon, formerly Duke University, USA) and Dr. [Swalpa Kumar Roy](https://scholar.google.com/citations?hl=en&user=1WVrFGwAAAAJ) (Associate Professor, Tezpur University). Currently I am working remotely at **i3 Research Lab, Harvard Medical School** under supervision of [Yangming Ou](https://labs.childrenshospital.org/yangming.ou) (Associate Professor of Radiology, Harvard Medical School). I am researching and developing medical LLMs for domain specific question answering and automated clinical report understanding using advanced language modeling.
